3. `lv-tok-terminal` checked-in board (`png-checked-in/lv-tok-terminal.png`, sha256 `f7e198eb…` from `frame-index.json`). Sampled pixels, not a new swatch: light preview ground `#EEF5F2`, failure-row fill flattens to `#F7E7E2` (55% `#FFDAD6` over `#EEF5F2` is about `#F7E6E3`), failure word `#410002`, stroke `#BA1A1A`, dark ground `#101615`. 11-13 section compare of this board is before=after (`READY-REFERENCES.md`). Not a d474 delta.
4. Product frames `lv-and-04-term-agent` / `lv-and-04-term-shell` / `lv-ios-04-term` are historic dark depictions. Sampled orig and checked-in Android log ground is `#0E1513`; iOS ground is `#000000`. `final-rev-CHANGES.md` says the 2026-11-06 pass did not redraw iOS terminal. `frame-index.json` still labels these frames `theme: light` and points the iOS frame at the light-board SPEC. Follow the SPEC, not those dark pixels.
5. No d474 transition frames and no mux D. `lv-and-05-transition` / `lv-ios-05-transition` stay with the router owner.

`#FFDAD6` at 55% over `#EEF5F2` is the SPEC fill. Theme already stores that as `failureFill` and the flattened peach as `failureWash` (`#F7E7E2`, `LuviaThemeTest.failureWashAndCardFollowTheTerminalWow`). The page may use `failureFill`. It must not add a third shade.
